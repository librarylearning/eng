PICTURES FOLDER
===============

Put pictures for words and stories here (for later modules).

  - Use only pictures you took yourself, or free-to-use pictures
    (for example from openclipart.org, or emoji).
  - Use small letters and dashes in names: bus-stop.jpg, injera.png
  - Keep each picture small (under 150 KB) so the site loads fast.
  - Photos of her own world (her kitchen, her bus stop, her
    library) are often the best pictures of all.
